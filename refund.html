Refund Policy

How refunds and cancellations work. Last updated: October 2025.

1. Before work begins

If you cancel before design or development starts, you receive a full refund of the deposit, minus third-party costs already paid, such as a domain or hosting plan.

2. After work has started

Refunds are based on work already completed. The amount covering completed work is retained and the remaining balance is refunded.

3. Delivered and approved projects

Once a project is completed and approved, or the website has launched, the amount paid is not refundable.

4. Third-party services

Domains, hosting and plugins are not refundable once purchased.

5. If we cancel

If we cannot complete an agreed project, you receive a full refund for work that has not been delivered.

6. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through the same payment method, usually the same Mobile Money number, within 7 to 14 working days after approval.

7. How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email apexcyberlinkco@gmail.com with your name, payment reference, service and reason.

8. Payments you should never make

We will never ask for your Mobile Money PIN or bank card details. Only pay the number published on the Payments page and confirm on WhatsApp.
